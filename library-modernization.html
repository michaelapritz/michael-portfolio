Electronic Resources Audit

Finding the Access NASA Already Had

In preparation for the Alma/Primo migration, I audited NASA Langley’s journal and e-book subscriptions, access rights, and coverage with publishers and providers. The audit surfaced entitlements that had gone unnoticed and prepared cleaner journal data for migration.
